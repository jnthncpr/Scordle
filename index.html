Scoring — the twist

  • GREEN +10 the first time a letter lands correct in that spot. Guess the same green again later and it's worth 0 — no points for duplicate greens.
  • YELLOW +5 every single time, even if you've seen that letter go yellow before.
  • GRAY +0 always.

💡 Strategy: Solving fast caps your score low. Once you know a letter's in the word, try it in a different wrong spot before you lock it in — every fresh yellow is another 5 points. You've only got 6 guesses, so don't wait too long to actually solve it!
